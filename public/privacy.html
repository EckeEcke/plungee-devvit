Plungee Privacy Policy

Effective: 7 November 2026

This policy covers Plungee, the Reddit game registered as plungee-game. Plungee is operated by u/Triggerscore. For privacy questions and requests, open the Reddit profile of u/Triggerscore and send a private message or see Help & Support.

Data we use

Reddit supplies your Reddit user ID and username when you use authenticated features. Personal challenge previews may display the challenge creator's and viewer's public Reddit avatar. We use this information to associate verified scores, published levels, reports and saved drafts with the correct account. We do not ask for your password, email address, real name or payment details.

Gameplay records may contain input replays, verified times, death counts, character appearance, timestamps, community play statistics and moderation reports. Publishing a level stores its title and layout. Posting a result stores a reference to the Reddit comment; Plungee does not cache its comment body.

Purpose and visibility

We use these records to run the game, verify results, provide leaderboards and replays, save drafts, moderate community levels and honor deletion requests. Published levels and their author attribution are visible to other players. Other players' challenge results and replays remain hidden until the challenge closes; the released leaderboard shows the top five runs and their appearances. Moderators can review community-level reports and take action.

Storage and recipients

Account-associated game data is stored using Reddit's Devvit Redis. Device-local browser storage holds campaign progress, character and audio preferences, editor preferences and your own best replays. These local settings do not synchronize between devices. Plungee does not send your game records to an external advertising or analytics service or sell your data. Reddit operates the hosting platform and processes platform data under its own policies.

Retention

Post scores and leaderboards expire after 90 days, refreshed when an improved score is saved for that post. The user score index expires 90 days after the user's last improved score. Drafts and certain posting references expire after 90 days; active community-run payloads expire after one day. Published community-level data, aggregate play records and moderation records may remain until removed or deleted. Device-local data remains until cleared by you, your browser or the app's deletion function.

Deletion and your choices

Use Delete saved data in the main menu to remove your app-held scores, replays, drafts, reports, creator-owned level data and related local settings. Your published community levels then stop being playable. This does not delete Reddit posts or comments; use Reddit's controls for those. Deleting a Reddit post or comment triggers cleanup of its related app-held content and references.

A daily cleanup begins at 03:00 UTC to check for deleted accounts. Confirmed deleted accounts are removed from app-held identity records and attribution, while undeleted public levels can remain without identifying attribution. An inconclusive missing-profile response is checked again; accounts that remain unresolvable for 30 days are cleaned up as a retention fallback, which can also affect suspended accounts. API errors do not authorize deletion. Older unindexed private records may remain until their existing expiry where Redis provides no way to discover them.

You may request access, correction or deletion through Help & Support. Depending on your location, you may have additional data-protection rights. We may ask you to establish control of the relevant Reddit account before handling a request. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised effective date. Material changes to how game data is used will be explained in the app or its Reddit release information.
