Help & Support

Game problems and feature requests

Contact Plungee's developer u/Triggerscore on Reddit to report a problem or request a feature. Include your device, browser or Reddit app, level name and steps to reproduce it. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information, and do not publicly share unreleased challenge routes or replays.

Privacy and account questions

Open the Reddit profile of u/Triggerscore and use the message or chat controls for account, access, correction and deletion requests. Please keep these requests private. You can also use Delete saved data in Plungee's main menu to remove your app-held data.

Report a level or inappropriate content

Use Report in the Community levels list. Subreddit moderators can review levels through Moderate levels. For Reddit posts or comments, use Reddit's own reporting and moderator-contact controls.

Controls

Use the arrow keys or A/D on desktop, or hold the left or right side on mobile. R restarts a level on desktop. Character, audio and campaign preferences are stored on your current device.
